Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

My Beauty Product ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website and online store. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you:

  • Order & Contact Details: Name, delivery address, WhatsApp phone number, city, and email address when you make a purchase or contact us.
  • Shopping Preferences: Cart items, wishlist selections, and AI Beauty Advisor queries saved locally in your browser.
  • Payment Details: We do not store sensitive payment card credentials. Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, and EasyPaisa transactions are processed securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process, ship, and deliver your cosmetics orders to your address across Pakistan.
  • To notify you via SMS or WhatsApp regarding tracking and delivery progress.
  • To provide bespoke customer care and AI Beauty Advisor shade matching.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders and maintain platform security.

4. Data Sharing & Courier Partners

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary contact and delivery details with licensed courier partners (such as TCS Express, Leopards, or Call Courier) strictly for fulfilling your parcel deliveries.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We employ industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and access controls to keep your personal data secure.
