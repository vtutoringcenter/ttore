Are all your products 100% Halal certified? +
Yes, absolutely. Every single product in our catalog is 100% halal-certified. We never use animal fats, carmine (crushed insects), or ethyl alcohol. Certified under strict Pakistani halal standards.
Can I perform Wudu and pray Salah while wearing your makeup? +
Our mineral formulations are breathable and free of any Najis (impure) elements. While water must reach bare skin during ritual ablution (wudu), our formulas wipe off cleanly and gently with mild water or micellar wipes.
Are your products cruelty-free? +
Yes, 100%. We never test our formulas or raw ingredients on animals, and we only partner with cruelty-free ethical suppliers worldwide.

How long does delivery take across Pakistan? +
Orders in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi typically arrive in 1 to 3 business days. Other cities and remote areas take 3 to 5 business days via trusted couriers (TCS / Call Courier / Leopards).

We offer FREE delivery nationwide on all orders above PKR 2,500! For orders under PKR 2,500, a flat shipping fee of PKR 200 applies.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? +
Yes! Cash on Delivery is available across all cities and towns in Pakistan. You only pay when your parcel is delivered to your doorstep.

You can visit our Track Order Page and enter your Order ID (e.g. MBP-2026-XXXX) or phone number to see real-time updates!
